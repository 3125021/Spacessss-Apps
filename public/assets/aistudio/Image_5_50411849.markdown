## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the precision, wonder, and institutional gravitas of a world-class planetary observatory and NASA exploratory research initiative. It bridges high-resolution planetary science with cutting-edge public museum curation. The interface must feel like an operational mission control terminal blended with an editorial digital exhibition.

The design movement combines **Deep Space Glassmorphism** with **Technical High-Precision Futurism**:
- **Tone:** Authoritative, visionary, meticulous, and awe-inspiring.
- **Visual Weight:** Weightless, expansive cosmic depth counterbalanced by laser-sharp telemetry overlays, micro-borders, and high-density informational readouts.
- **Lighting Model:** Dark-mode native, illuminated by synthetic telemetry beacons, bioluminescent accents, and faint atmospheric limb glows.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in planetary observation data, calibrated against space agency telemetry standards:

- **Deep Space Foundations:** `#030712` (Cosmic Black base canvas), `#050B14` (Deep Space secondary backdrop), and `#0B132B` (Deep Void Navy container layer).
- **Primary Energy (`#00F0FF` - Electric Cyan):** Used for primary interactive triggers, active timeline nodes, live telemetry data streams, and focused states.
- **Secondary Atmospheric (`#38BDF8` - Bioluminescent Azure):** Used for atmospheric limb contours, glass highlight borders, structural metadata, and secondary interactive states.
- **Tertiary Orbital (`#F59E0B` - Orbital Gold):** Used sparingly for critical mission milestones, solar events, geological epochs, and high-priority observational anomalies.
- **Deep Space Accent (`#8B5CF6` - Deep Space Violet):** Applied as faint, ambient radial backdrops behind deep-time visualizers and spectroscopy charts.

### Transparency & Glow Rules
- Glass surfaces use `rgba(11, 19, 43, 0.65)` layered over deep space canvases.
- Micro-borders employ `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.2)` default, shifting to `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.5)` on hover or focus.
- Interactive glows must maintain a tight radius (`box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.35)`) to avoid muddy contrast.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes clear operational boundaries between narrative storytelling and mission data:

- **Space Grotesk (Headlines & Epoch Identifiers):** Carries structural tension and futuristic geometry. Use uppercase sparingly for major era headings, paired with generous tracking.
- **Inter (Narrative Body & Scientific Explanations):** Ensures maximum legibility across long-form planetary histories, curatorial essays, and artifact briefs.
- **JetBrains Mono (Telemetry, Coordinates, Data Readouts, & Timestamps):** All numerical readouts, geological ages (`e.g., 4.543 Ga ± 1%`), coordinate grids (`LAT/LONG/ALT`), sensor badges, and status codes must render strictly in this monospace family.

Numeric figures in tables and real-time streams must use tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`).

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial architecture balances expansive cosmic voids with high-density scientific instrumentation:

- **Grid Architecture:** 12-column responsive fluid grid anchored by a 1440px desktop standard and max-canvas clamp at 1800px. Content rests inside precise structural margins to evoke instrument frames.
- **Adaptive Breakpoints:**
  - **Desktop (1200px+):** Full 12-column layout. Timeline rails, planetary viewport, and telemetry side-drawers coexist simultaneously. Margin is `3rem` (48px); gutter is `1.5rem` (24px).
  - **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column layout. Side telemetry panels collapse into sliding bottom sheets; hero globe view claims persistent central priority. Margin is `2rem` (32px); gutter is `1.25rem` (20px).
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout. Monospace telemetry collapses to compact single-row ribbons. Margin is `1.25rem` (20px); gutter is `1rem` (16px).
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows strict multiples of 8px (with 4px for tight badge metadata). Use `space-2xl` between planetary stages to deliver spatial reverence, while internal card data uses tight `space-sm` and `space-md` gaps.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through optical atmospheric layering rather than drop shadows:

- **Level 0 (Cosmic Floor):** `#030712` canvas, optionally layered with subtle starlight noise (0.02 opacity) or faint orbital vector rings.
- **Level 1 (Sub-surface Panels):** Background `#050B14` with a 1px solid border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`).
- **Level 2 (Active Glassmorphic Decks):** Background `rgba(11, 19, 43, 0.55)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a directional 1px perimeter gradient border running from `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.35)` (top-left) to `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.05)` (bottom-right).
- **Level 3 (Interactive Floating Inspect Overlays):** Background `rgba(11, 19, 43, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`, a crisp 1px border (`rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.4)`), and an atmospheric glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px -4px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.2)`.
- **Level 4 (Critical Mission Modals & Data Beacons):** Crisp borders with double luminosity rings (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.6), 0 0 32px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.25)`).

## Shapes

The shape system is deliberately constrained to **Soft (`roundedness: 1`)** to maintain an instrumental, laboratory equipment aesthetic:

- **Base Radius (0.25rem / 4px):** Applied to telemetry badges, buttons, tooltips, slider thumbs, and monospaced code blocks.
- **Container Radius (0.5rem / 8px):** Applied to glassmorphism cards, observation modals, timeline scrubbers, and visualizer modules.
- **Nested Inner Geometry (0.25rem / 4px):** Retains strict concentric geometry with parent cards.
- **Telemetry Notches & Corner Marks:** Component corners may employ 1px optical reticle brackets (`L-brackets`) at the extremities of data viewports to amplify the scientific viewfinder aesthetic.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Telemetry Button:** Filled with `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.15)`, 1px border of `#00F0FF`, text in `#00F0FF` (`JetBrains Mono`, uppercase, tracked +0.08em). On hover: solid `#00F0FF` background, `#030712` text, with `box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.4)`.
- **Secondary Ghost Button:** Background `rgba(11, 19, 43, 0.4)`, 1px border of `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.2)`, text in `#E2E8F0`. Hover shifts border to `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.6)`.

### Telemetry Badges & Epoch Chips
- Compact pills or 4px-radius badges featuring a 6px pulsing status dot (`#00F0FF` for real-time/orbital, `#F59E0B` for planetary events).
- Micro-typography rendered in `JetBrains Mono` at `label-caps` size.
- Subtle glass fill with a 1px border colored dynamically according to telemetry tier.

### Cards & Observational Modules
- Glassmorphic panels with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and perimeter glow borders.
- Top-left card header always features a monospace category index (e.g., `SEC-04 // ATMOSPHERIC CO2`) alongside the title in `Space Grotesk`.
- Technical corner reticles (subtle crosshairs or corner tick marks) on high-priority analytical cards.

### Input Fields & Sliders
- **Inputs:** Dark recessed base (`#050B14`), 1px border of `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.25)`, glowing to `#00F0FF` on focus. Monospace label floating above in uppercase.
- **Chronological Scrubber / Timeline Slider:** Thin 2px rail in `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.2)` with glowing progress segment in `#00F0FF`. Thumb is a precision reticle target box with continuous timestamp readouts.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Crisp square (checkbox) and diamond-oriented (radio) geometries.
- Inactive: 1px border of `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.3)`.
- Active: Electric cyan glow fill with high-contrast dark cosmic glyph.

### Specialized Scientific Visualizer Modules
- **Spectrogram & Geological Stratum Gauges:** High-contrast step-ladder charts with alternating tick lines.
- **Orbital Epoch Navigator:** Sticky chronological progress bar pinned along the display axis with interactive epoch checkpoints that expand into glass preview cards upon scrubbing.
